Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Consumers have the right to:

• Right to Safety

• Right to Information

• Right to Choose

• Right to Seek Redressal

• Right to Consumer Education

Complaints can be filed online through the e-Jagriti Portal.

District Commission generally handles lower-value claims according to the applicable pecuniary limits under current law.

Every complaint should include:

Invoice

Proof of payment

Communication with seller

Product photographs
